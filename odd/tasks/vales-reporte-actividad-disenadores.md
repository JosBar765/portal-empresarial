## Owner spec
1. Counters: the same five as tiendas/asesores + EFECTIVIDAD (entregados / no entregados over the whole taller) + percentage of on-time deliveries (entregas a tiempo).
2. ALL percentage counters of the Reportes allow two decimals (Urgentes included; today they are integers).
3. Grouped bar chart per designer: bar 1 = vales assigned to the designer; bar 2 = vales delivered by the designer, STACKED with the late deliveries in RED with their number inside (like urgentes in the asesores chart).
4. A column next to the chart: individual effectiveness % and late-delivery %; a letter in each column header and a legend explaining each letter.
5. Pressing the chart applies filters (like the asesores chart); counters are pressable too (existing counter filters).
6. The PDF asks whether to print with the applied filters (existing behavior for tiendas/asesores).

## Decisions
- OWNER: option 1 for shared vales: a vale shared by designers A and B counts as delivered for A when A's own part is approved (all A's rows, within the taller scope, approved by the Encargado at the cut), even if B is pending (legacy `_bloqueDisenadores` semantics). Assigned = vales where the designer has a row in scope.
- MINE (to confirm): taller-wide counters count UNIQUE vales with the report's existing vale-level definition (`terminadoTaller`); EFECTIVIDAD counter = `recibidos.total / ingresados` (entregados ÷ asignados of the whole scope, all vales of the window; `null` with no base, shown as «—»); A TIEMPO = (entregados − con atraso) ÷ entregados. Individual column: E = entregados ÷ asignados of the designer; T = entregas tarde ÷ entregados of the designer (the counter says «a tiempo», the column says «tardías», as the owner asked).
- MINE: percentages rounded to at most 2 decimals, trailing zeros trimmed («50 %», «33.33 %»), decimal point per `es-GT`; one pure helper on the frontend and one on the server/PDF instead of the four duplicated roundings.
- MINE: chart colors from design tokens: assigned = primary blue, delivered on time = green (success), late = red (danger); legend names them. Numbers inside the red segment even for a single late delivery.
- MINE: pressing: name or «Asignados» bar = all the designer's vales (todos); the on-time part of «Entregados» = delivered (`entregados`); the red part = delivered late only (`tarde`). The group counters come from `porGrupo[id][modo]` (variants todos / urgentes / entregados / tarde); the desglose uses the existing counter-filter states (`entregados`, `entregadosConAtraso`) on top of the group focus. The PDF focus param is `disenadorDesglose` plus `desgloseEstado` for the entregados/tarde modes.
- Group «Sin diseñador asignado» (id 0) is a normal group: no truthiness checks on ids.
- Names in the chart use `nombresConRol` (the same label as the selector).
- PDF counters/chart stay those of the general query; the new cards and the grouped chart with its E/T column and legend are drawn in the PDF too.

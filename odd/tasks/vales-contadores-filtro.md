# Vales: pressing a counter filters the desglose (tiendas and asesores reports)

Branch: `feat/vales-reportes-2` (uncommitted, mixed with earlier work). Owner request (2026-11-20): pressing a counter applies a filter to the vales listed; pressing it again removes it. Filters: urgente, sin entregar, atrasados, atrasados anteriores, atrasados actuales, entregados, entregados con atraso, entregados sin atraso. Design: same cards (mockup waived: behavior on existing UI). Frontend only: the generated payload already carries what is needed (sections, `urgente`, `diasTarde`, `atrasadosVigentes`).

## Decisions (mine, to be confirmed by the owner)
- State filters are EXCLUSIVE among themselves (one at a time; pressing another switches): sinEntregar, atrasados, atrasadosAnteriores, atrasadosActuales, entregados, entregadosConAtraso, entregadosSinAtraso. «Urgente» is an independent toggle that COMBINES with the state filter (intersection). Both are view state, never persisted.
- Meaning (matches each counter's definition): sinEntregar = section noEntregados; atrasados = sections atrasados (actuales) + atrasadosVigentes (anteriores); atrasadosAnteriores = atrasadosVigentes only; atrasadosActuales = section atrasados only; entregados = section entregados; con/sin atraso = entregados with `diasTarde > 0` / `diasTarde === 0`; urgente = vales with `urgente === true` in the PERIOD sections (the Urgentes counter is relative to «Ingresados», which excludes the anteriores), so «atrasados anteriores» are hidden while «urgente» is on.
- Combines with the bar focus (tienda/asesor): bar focus first, counter filter second. The counter numbers do not change (they describe the whole or focused set); the active counters are highlighted (`aria-pressed`) and a chip row above the desglose shows the active filters with ×.
- OWNER CHANGE (same day, after T1+T2): the PDF flow asks whether to print WITH the view filters applied. The "view filters" are the bar focus AND the counter filters. When any is active the "include the desglose?" modal becomes three choices: (A) with the filters applied (the focused group + counter filters + urgent), (B) the full desglose (ignoring bar and counters), (C) without desglose; each option shows how many vales it prints and warns above 100 rows; with no view filter active the modal stays as today (Sí / No). The PDF counters and chart stay those of the general query (as before). Server: new PDF-only params `desgloseEstado` (one of the 7 states) and `desgloseUrgentes=1`, applied by a PURE backend module that mirrors the frontend filter (cross-checked by a test on the same fake data); the PDF header line states the applied filter.
- No fetch: pressing only repaints from the held payload. With `desgloseOmitido` (more than 5000 vales) the counters are not pressable.
- The counter filter is cleared when another report is selected; it survives repaints and regeneration of the same report.
